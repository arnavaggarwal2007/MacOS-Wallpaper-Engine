Questions, troubleshooting, and how to reach the developer.

How to get help

Email support: arnevaggarrwal@gmail.com

Please include your macOS version, Deskface version (Settings → System), and a short description of what you expected versus what happened. Screenshots help when the menu bar or wallpaper preview is involved.

Common questions

  • Where is the app window? Deskface is a menu bar app. Click the Deskface icon near the clock to open the main window. On first launch, Home shows a short welcome card with the same tip.
  • What video formats work? Local MP4 and MOV files. You can also use local HTML or an https:// URL for optional web wallpapers.
  • Does it change the lock screen? No. Deskface sets the desktop wallpaper only.
  • Is my media uploaded? No. Wallpapers stay on your Mac. See the privacy policy.
  • Is this Wallpaper Engine for Steam? No. Deskface is not affiliated with, or compatible with, Wallpaper Engine on Steam.

Bug reports

Prefer email for support. If you use GitHub, you may also open an issue on the project tracker (optional; not required for App Store support).

Privacy

Deskface is local-first: no account, no analytics, no generative AI. Full details: Privacy Policy — Deskface.
