Privacy Policy — Loopscape

Last updated: August 31, 2026 · Product: Loopscape (macOS)
Applies to the Mac App Store release and any future direct-download build unless a superseding policy is published.

Summary

Loopscape is a local-first wallpaper application. It does not require an account, does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not use generative AI features.

Generative AI

Loopscape contains no chatbots, image generation, or other generative AI features. Wallpaper content comes from your local video files, your local HTML files, or URLs you choose for web wallpapers.

Information we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses, or account credentials — there are no accounts.
  • Location data.
  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Usage analytics or telemetry. There are no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Generative-AI prompts or outputs, because the app has no AI features.
  • Your wallpaper media. Videos, images, and web content stay on your Mac.

Information stored on your device

Loopscape stores preferences and state locally on your Mac, including:

  • Selected wallpaper paths and security-scoped bookmarks, so access survives a relaunch.
  • Collections, desktop setups, library folder roots, and related settings.
  • Optional thumbnail caches used for library browsing.
  • Power and performance preferences.

This data stays on your device unless you back up your Mac with your own tools, such as Time Machine.

Network use

  • Local video wallpapers play without any network access.
  • Optional web wallpapers: if you enter or select a remote URL, Loopscape loads that URL over the network at your request. Only https:// addresses and local HTML files are accepted. The privacy practices of those third-party sites are outside our control, and we do not record which URLs you load.
  • Mac App Store builds receive updates through Apple. There is no third-party auto-updater.
  • Future direct-download builds may check for updates. This policy will be updated to describe those checks before that ships.

Screen saver and lock-screen helpers (future)

If screen saver or lock-screen export features ship, they will use local media and system settings you control. Preferences shared between the main app and a screen saver extension may use an App Group container on your Mac — still local, and not uploaded to us.

Children's privacy

Loopscape is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

  • Apple: App Store distribution, any crash reporting you enable in macOS system settings, and payment processing if optional In-App Purchases (for example a tip) are offered in future.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

  • Remove library folders, clear the thumbnail cache, and delete the app to remove local preferences.
  • Revoke file access in macOS System Settings, under Privacy & Security.
  • Uninstall the screen saver module, when shipped, in System Settings under Screen Saver.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date will change, and material changes for App Store builds will be reflected in App Store Connect privacy labels when required.
