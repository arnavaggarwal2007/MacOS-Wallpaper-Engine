Deskloop

Live wallpapers for your Mac, from your own video files.

What it does

  • Plays local MP4 and MOV files as animated desktop wallpapers, behind your icons.
  • Assigns a different wallpaper to each display, or one across all of them.
  • Organizes wallpapers into collections and saved desktop setups.
  • Indexes folders on your Mac as a browsable local library.
  • Offers quick modes and menu bar controls for fast switching.
  • Pauses on battery and exposes explicit performance profiles.
  • Optionally renders a web page as a wallpaper, limited to https:// addresses and local HTML files.

Privacy

Deskloop is local-first. There is no account, no analytics, and no generative AI. Your wallpaper files never leave your Mac. Read the full privacy policy.
